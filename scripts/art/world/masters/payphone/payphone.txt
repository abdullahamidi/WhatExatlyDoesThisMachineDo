# while it fogs) with the glass fogged: flat #5a5f63 over the whole tally area (the procedural marks draw crisp on
# it; T8 hand-off: WORLD_MARKS fog colour #ddc9a3), a #7d8286 condensation row at the glass bottom (row 37, under
# the last tally row), the shadow column #3e4246, and two clear drip runs (#2b2e31, x 13 rows 20-37 and x 14 rows
# 27-37) only in the gap between the two tally columns. restored = dark with every rust patch painted out to its
# iron neighbour and the glass whole: two parallel 45 degree reflections (#5a5f63 and #3e4246, upper left, #7d8286
# tips) under the marks; hood lamp off. The cradle on the unit stays empty in every frame: the docked handset is
# world.receiver onHook, drawn on the unit's outer side (README AD3b hand-off: WORLD_ENV.receiverOnHookArt).
