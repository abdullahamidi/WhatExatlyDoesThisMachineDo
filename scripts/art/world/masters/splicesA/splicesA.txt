# WP28a Splice objects, eras I-II (AD1, Art Director). ONE uniform-cell sheet: cell 32x24 art px, single row, 20 cells,
# every object bottom-centered: pivot (0.5, 1) = cell (16, 24) = the object's feet / its placement point. Codes:
# scripts/art/world/pal.py. Build: python -I scripts/art/world/build_sheet.py splicesA
# Gate: python -I scripts/art/world/check_sheet.py splicesA (full 1 px ink ring on every cell except the bicycle cells:
# thin 1 px tubes, spokes and stand, sheets.py `no_ring`; the bicycle silhouette is counted 8-connected, `conn8`).
# Single row like the street lamp and clock (street furniture of the old world, lit from the upper left): no era rows.
# Pre-finale cells use no green or blue and no warm yellow (#f0a848 / #ffd27a); lit bulbs and the lit dial are #d9822b
# on rust (the lamp's lit rule). Only the `*Whole` cells (postgame, restored world) use #f0a848 / #ffd27a. No letters.
#  0-4   bicycle 26x15: dead (chain off the ring, hanging), spin0-2 (chain on; rear wheel turns BACKWARDS: the 4 half
#        spokes step +30 deg counter-clockwise per frame = 120 deg real, the pedal cranks follow at 60 deg = the 2:1 chain
#        ratio, so the 3-frame loop is seamless), whole (clean paint, bright bell). A child's roadster on a rear stand: the
#        rear wheel sits 1 px off the ground on the stand leg, so it can spin; the bell is the 2 px dome on the bar.
#  5-10  barrel organ 28x24: arched pipe front (five tin pipes, the tallest in the middle) in a wood case with a gilt
#        cornice and crest, on a barrow (handles to the left = the outer side on the left wing, one leg, one wheel).
#        dead (one pipe missing, the crank down), crank0-3 (the crank on the case's right end: up, right, down, left;
#        clockwise), whole (gilt cornice and crest).
#  11-14 valve radio 16x20: cathedral cabinet with a fretted grille on a pine crate. dark, litA / litB (the dial glows
#        #d9822b / #c98552, the needle drifts left / right, the grille cloth catches the light), whole (warm dial).
#  15-18 bulb arrow sign 30x22: a sheet-metal arrow on two iron legs, bent down at the left, the head pointing at the
#        ground; 17 bulb sockets. dark, halfA / halfB (alternate halves lit), whole (every bulb warm). No letters.
#  19    clockLit 12x12: the street clock's face lit from inside (dial #c98552, rim #ad6236, ticks #8c4a2a, the crack
#        kept), a stacked overlay on world.clock; NO hands (the renderer draws them, 4:41). Cell (10..21, 12..23):
#        its center (16, 18) sits 6 art px above the pivot, so the overlay's pivot goes 6 art px (12 world px) BELOW
#        the clock's dial center (clock foot - 72 world px): pivot = clock foot - 60 world px.
