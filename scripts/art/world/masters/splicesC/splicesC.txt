#        tonearm jumps 1 px), whole (polished horn).
#  11-14 wind chimes on their lamp arm 14x18 (overlay on the lamp): the arm continues the lamp's ladder bar (cell rows

#  15-19 weathervane 16x18 (overlay on the booth apex: cell cols 11-12 = booth cols 13-14, cell row 17 sits on the apex):
#        finial ball (the crow's perch top), spindle, an arrow with a tail plate on its pivot (row 7), a compass cross

#        it always stops there), whole (gilt, points right).
#  20-23 coin-return flap 4x4 (overlay on the booth's phone unit, booth cols 24-27 rows 25-28): closed (dead), rattle0
