# WP28b Splice objects, eras III-V, tall set (AD3-OBJ, Art Director). ONE uniform-cell sheet: cell 18x56 art px, single row,
# 22 cells, every object bottom-centred: pivot (0.5, 1) = cell (9, 56) = the object's feet / its placement point (the kite:
# see below). Codes: scripts/art/world/pal.py. Build: python -I scripts/art/world/build_sheet.py splicesB
# Gate: python -I scripts/art/world/check_sheet.py splicesB (full 1 px ink ring except the `no_ring` frames: the plume and
# the kite's 1 px string, tail and bows; `parts_frames`: the coughed drops, the plume wisps and the lifted stones float
# free; `violet_frames`: the era V violetLight rim on the lifted stones). Light from the upper left. No green, no blue,
# no letters. Wrong colours of the woken frames: rust, amber, white-hot, era V violet; #f0a848 / #ffd27a mostly in *Whole.
#  0-4   hand pump 12x24 (+ spout and drops; cols 1-17): a village pitcher pump on a stone well cap. Handle on the left
#        (pivot boss on a bracket over the cap), spout on the right, its mouth at col 16 (feet + 14..16 world px):
#        dead (handle hanging, rod up, rust), up / down (the stroke; the plunger rod shows above the cap on the down
#        stroke), cough (down + drops falling in col 16 and a splash on the ground row), whole (clean iron, brass
#        spout, handle up).
#  5-7   factory whistle on a post 6x44 (rows 12-55): a domed brass bell over a bowl (the slot at row 21 is where the
#        steam leaves), valve, lever with a pull chain down the post, the steam pipe up the post's left side into the
#        valve. dead (tarnished, lever up), blow (lever pulled, the plume billows from the slot up past the bell; the
#        plume is IN the cell, SteamFx is not used), whole (polished).
#  8-11  bell on a timber gallows frame 18x28 (rows 28-55): still (tarnished, hanging plumb), swingL / swingR (the bell
#        turns 1 px at the waist and the lip about its headstock axle, the clapper lags: never all the way), whole.
#  12-15 kite 9x10 + string, an overlay on the lamp: cell row 55 = the lamp's glass bottom row (lamp row 13), cell col
#        k = lamp col k - 3, the finial at cell (8-9, 46). tangled (hanging upside down by its string from the
#        finial against the hood's right side, a torn panel, the tail caught too), flutter0 / flutter1 (risen 20 / 40
#        art px on a string pulled straight DOWN to the finial, the tail floating UP: gravity wrong), whole (flying
#        up-left in a breeze, the string bellies down to the finial, amber paper).
#  16-21 cairn 14x16: bedrock slab + four stones. stack (dead), lift1-4 (top stone first; they hang 16, 12, 8, 4 art px
#        above their rest and never fall back; violetLight rim on each lifted stone), whole (settled, a pebble on top).
