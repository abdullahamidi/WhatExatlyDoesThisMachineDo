## Goal
The world plays like a recorded Tuesday running forward: every denizen keeps doing different, canon-true things on its own irregular
clock, small interactions tie them to each other and to the props, light and weather drift with the day as the tape fills, and every
era, tier and purchase change happens on screen over seconds to minutes. Eras IV and V stay inside their canon (unison, the stare) and
still read alive: never static, never looping. The machine stays the focus.
