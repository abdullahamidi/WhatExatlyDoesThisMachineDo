## Goal
Answer the three complaints without breaking the WP27 click curve or "every energy stays useful":
- **No dead wait.** Purchases come about twice as often, in smaller steps, at the same total time: the tired player's p90 wait drops
  from 1:56 to about 1:02 and the longest from 6:08 to about 2:33. A **NEXT BUY** line says what comes next and roughly when.
  **Splices** sit inside the remaining waits as a second purchase track, and **Finds** give the eye a reason to scan the stage
  about every 70 s.
- **A warm world.** Every denizen and every woken object answers a poke (varied, short, escalating, then politely unavailable). The
  cast roams the whole wing and the empty yard, gathers at woken objects and reacts to what the machine does: faults, Finds, big
  buys and era changes.
- **The machine's reach.** A Splice runs a visible line from the machine to a dead object of the old world and wakes it, "partly
  wrong". Crank clicks, output level and big purchases show in the ground, the dust, the wires and the lamp, plus one signature
  effect per era.

## Director decisions (binding; copied from the dispatch, shortened only where the text repeats)
- **D1 No dead wait (pacing).** Measure first. Metric: purchase gap = time between consecutive purchases of anything (tree levels,
  Splices) in the real `runGreedy` harness, for profile A (4 cps, catches faults as in WP27) and profile B ("tired of clicking": 4 cps
  for 5 min, then 0.5 cps, catches no faults and no finds); B is the main target. Also report the "event gap": the longest stretch
  with no purchase and no fault / find on screen. Targets: B p90 gap cut by ≥ 40 % and B max gap ≤ 4 min outside the final alignment;
  A p90 ≤ 75 s and max ≤ 2.5 min. Keep A's PF within ±10 % of 250:09, B / A 1.2-1.6, demo A substation within ±15 %, every WP27
  click-curve gate (`clickCurve.sim`) and every "all energies stay useful" gate (or explicit re-targets with reasons). Levers:
  cost-growth / amount splits, cheaper intermediate steps, Splices in the gaps, Finds. UI: a NEXT readout (next affordable buy + ETA)
  if nothing like it exists.
- **D2 Splices.** The machine runs a line out to a dead object of the prior world and wakes it, "partly wrong" (the world gets
  stranger, not better, until the finale). 3-4 per era (12-18 total; the demo has the era I-II ones). Bought by clicking the object
  in the world (hover: name, cost, effect in plain words) and from a keyboard-reachable list. One-time purchases (a few may have 2-3
  levels); cost in an energy of the current era, sized to land inside the waits. Visible: a cable from the machine or the pylon line
  to the object, pulses on it scale with output (log), the object wakes and keeps a small live animation, denizens react to and
  gather at woken objects. Effect: small, varied, plain words. Save: v2 additive or techs in the levels map; old saves load.
  Prestige: Splices reset with the machine (the world goes dark again; one log line).
- **D3 Finds.** Now and then a denizen or the wind produces a find; a small glinting item lies on the ground near it, clickable for
  about 20-30 s (a free key listed in SETTINGS > KEYS; screen-reader announcement). Payout a few seconds of all output (smaller than
  a fault). Every Nth find is a relic: a one-line note from the prior world (FIELD NOTES or the system log; canon; never the
  machine's purpose). Spawn about 45-120 s while interactive with the fault silence rules; max 1 alive; missing costs nothing;
  Splices raise rate / payout. Unlock early in era I; in the demo.
- **D4 Poke-able denizens.** Every denizen and every woken object is clickable (hover cursor and a subtle highlight). A click gets a
  reaction from a per-era table: varied, never the same twice in a row, ≤ 2 s, with a sound; repeated pokes escalate in a funny
  way. Rare lines respect WORLD VOICES and the caps. No economy reward. Canon kept: the girl never moves on screen; Sensor 7 faces the
  camera; denizens never touch, overlap or walk through the machine; no line names the machine's function or contains "RESTOR".
  Canon relaxed: the crow may hop or make short flights between perches on the stage in any era; it never leaves the stage before
  the finale.
- **D5 Roaming and reacting.** Denizens move around the stage: more spots, walks to woken Splice objects and to each other, visible
  reactions to machine events (fault, big buy / new part, crank burst, era change). Max gap between visible actions of each roaming
  denizen ≤ 12 s; no two consecutive identical activities. World display-object and update budgets hold.
- **D6 The machine's reach.** (a) A crank click sends a small visible pulse into the ground / cables (throttled; reduced motion =
  none); (b) output level drives the world (pulse rate on the pylon line and the Splice cables, dust motes near the base, lamp
  brightness wobble; log-scaled, capped, ≤ 3 Hz); (c) one signature effect per era scaled by output (I vibration / dust, II arcs on
  wires and lights, III steam drifting across the scene, IV loose objects hum and puddles ripple, V small debris lifts); (d) a big
  purchase ripples into the world. The AD designs within `docs/plan/ART_BRIEF.md`; numbers live in `src/data`.
- **D7 Text, a11y, audio.** English; system lines in the machine's dry voice; plain words; keys: F faults (kept), a new key for

## Pacing set REC (D1; all numbers live in `src/data/`)
How it works (audit §3): a gap is cost divided by income, so a side activity cannot shorten it much (6 s of payout in a 180 s gap is
3 %). Only smaller steps move B's p90, which is a broad mass of 70-150 s gaps (103 of 434), not a few long ones. A half-step split of
a track (growth g → √g, effect / 2, maxLevel × 2, base so that levels 2k and 2k+1 cost what old level k cost) keeps the spend for the
same power. The cheapest-first greedy then buys more track steps before each unlock of the same energy, so the era II-V unlocks get
cheaper (× 0.65) to keep the gates in their windows. Splices priced in an energy with slack fill the remaining waits from era II on;
Finds fill the event gap.

## Finds (D3)
### State machine (pure, `src/simulation/finds.ts`; data `src/data/finds.ts`; one clock per run, the fault clock contract)
```
LOCKED    --(eligible step, dt > 0: eligibleSeconds += dt)--> LOCKED
LOCKED    --(wornMotor owned)--> ARMING
ARMING    --(first eligible unlocked step: eligibleSeconds += dt; nextIn = max(draw(), firstAfterS - eligibleSeconds))--> WAITING
WAITING   --(eligible steps: eligibleSeconds += dt; nextIn -= dt; nextIn <= 0)--> ALIVE { age 0, window, serial, relic }   event spawn
ALIVE     --(collectFind: payout or relic; nextIn = draw())--> WAITING                                                  event collect
ALIVE     --(eligible steps: eligibleSeconds += dt; age += dt; age >= window: missed += 1; nextIn = draw())--> WAITING   event expire
any state --(not eligible, or dt not a finite number > 0)--> unchanged: the same object
draw() = (intervalS[0] + (intervalS[1] - intervalS[0]) * u) * intervalFactor     // u = one nextUnit step of the clock rng
relic  = relicEvery > 0 && serial % relicEvery === relicEvery - 1                  // serials 4, 9, 14, ...
```
- At most one Find is alive; a Find and a fault may be alive together.
- The pure step never clamps; `GameSession.stepFinds` clamps `dt` to `FINDS.maxStepS` (0.25 s) first, as faults do.
- Never offline: the clock moves only through `stepFinds`, which the Find marker calls per frame; `applyOffline` never touches it.
- A page load, a new cycle and a reset start a fresh clock (eligibleSeconds 0), so the first Find comes ≥ 120 eligible s after it.

### Payout
`payout[r] = perSecond[r] > 0 ? payoutSeconds × perSecond[r] : 0` with `perSecond = session.rates.perSecond` (servo included,
Overdrive not), `payoutSeconds = FINDS.payoutS + Σ findSeconds.seconds × L` (6 s; 7 s after the Barrel Organ; 8 s after the Kite).
A relic pays nothing. `intervalFactor = Π findRate.factor ^ L` (× 0.85 after the Factory Whistle). Expected value for a catcher:
6 × 0.85 × 0.8 / 89.7 = 4.5 % of output (measured 5.4 % for A); faults are 11-16 %. B gets nothing.

### Eligibility (the fault gates plus one)
`faultEligible(gates)` (tab visible, no photo mode, TECH INDEX closed, no era card, no modal, no cycle transition, teaser off, no
demo card, `ending.phase === 'none'`, enabled) AND at least one Find spot of the current framing on screen AND `setFinds(true)`
(debug). While not eligible the clock is frozen, the marker hidden and the key does nothing. The research's "+5 s grace after a
silence" is not adopted (harness parity; the clock is frozen, not reset, so no burst follows a menu).

  answer), 4+ → level 3 (the leave answer). After a level-3 answer the target is "unavailable" for 20 s. **[R1]** Pokes in that

- Bags: per target per era, avoid the last 2 picks; never the same reaction twice in a row (unit test over 1000 pokes, seeds 1-5,

- Every answer ≤ 2 s (the leave walk excepted), plays its sound (shuffle bag of variants, avoid last 2, ±5 % pitch) and changes
