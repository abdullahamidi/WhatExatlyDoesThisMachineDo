## Goal
**[R1]** From the first minute on (Worn Motor; A's ME passive passes 4-cps clicks at 0:20), the machine (automation + auto-cranks)
carries progression, and manual clicks become a visible, optional bonus. Targets for players who click less:
- a player who clicks only for the first minute (0.5 cps from Motor Tuning 5 on) reaches the Generator Blueprint at ≤ 1.35× the
  active player's time;
- a player who mostly watches after era I (0.5 cps) finishes at ≤ 1.25× (seed: 1.17×).
The active player's era windows, the demo cap time and "all energies stay useful" hold.

## Why a passive buff alone cannot fix it (verified in code)
- `src/simulation/production/rates.ts:107-110`: `perClick.me = clickAdd × clickMult`; `perClick[r] = echo[r] × perSecond[r]` for r ≠ me.
- Echo sources (`src/data/technologies.ts`): dynamoCrank 1 s EE (`:210`), steamHammer 1 s PR (`:354`), echoCrank 2 s RS + ×5 ME
  (`:514-515`), inversionCrank 3 s XF (`:663`). ME click mults: reinforcedCrank ×2 (`:49`), sparkGap ×3 (`:240`), echoCrank ×5 →
  up to 16 × 30 = 480 ME per click.
- Manual share at c clicks/s = echo·c / (1 + echo·c): a 1 s echo at 4 cps = 80 %, whatever the passive level (the echo scales with passive).
- Audit: 4-cps clicks earn 74 % of era-I ME and 80 % of era II–III EE/PR. Dropping to 0.5 cps after era I makes substation 2.04× and PF
  1.73× slower; 0 cps after era I makes PF 2.15× slower. Crank Servo (`:488`, era IV, folded into perSecond at `rates.ts:113-115`) is the
  only reason eras IV–V are already passive-led (P1 era IV 1.30×, era V 0.98×).
