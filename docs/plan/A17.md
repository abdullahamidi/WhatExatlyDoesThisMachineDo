## 2. References → rules
- **Great forge bellows** ([beautifuliron](https://www.beautifuliron.com/forge_blowrFireH.htm), [Wareham forge](http://warehamforgeblog.blogspot.com/2008/01/bellows-reconstruction-2.html)): the boards hinge at the snout. Internal ribs hold the leather sides out. The upper board is pumped or weighted. Air goes snout → tuyere → fire.
  - In art: a wedge in side elevation, folds radiating from the snout, and a hose into a collar at the firebox foot (grate level).
  - Scale: 1 art px ≈ 2.5 cm, so the 46 px board ≈ 1.2 m.
- **Suspension insulator string** ([glassian](https://glassian.org/Suspension/index.html), [sciencedirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/engineering/pin-insulator)): cap-and-pin discs linked pin-to-cap and hung from the crossarm. The conductor is clamped below.
  - In art: the backfeed is a string under the gallery deck, with the lug at the bottom and a jumper up into a deck bushing.
- **Steam lance / soot-blower wall unit** ([merchantnavydecoded](https://www.merchantnavydecoded.com/soot-blower-in-boiler/), [allindustrialtraining](https://allindustrialtraining.com/soot-blower/)): a valve admits feed steam into a short lance through the wall, with the jet at the tip.
  - In art: the valve hangs under the deck and the riser enters its flanged bottom port.
- **House rules kept**: every part stands on or hangs from steel. Locked palette plus era accents. Alpha 0/255 only. No resampling. Cables and pipes end on drawn lugs and ports.
