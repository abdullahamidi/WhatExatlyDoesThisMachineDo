## D Decisions (planner)
1. **primaryFunction lands as data only.** Purchasable `unlock` with the `ending` marker; purchase = normal purchase event + toast `Primary function engaged.`;
   no sequence, plate stays frame 4, no state field. WP12 starts the finale on the purchase of a tech whose effects include `ending` and decides how a save
   with primaryFunction = 1 resumes. Greedy treats it like any unlock (its firstAt = game end).
2. **MACHINE_BOUNDS.top −616, not −608:** the aperture box spans rows −610..−583 (ART §4 clearances); −608 breaks the "every box inside bounds" invariant.
   RT 1120×624 (+63 % pixels vs Era IV 896×478); fit (margin 72) = min(1280/2384, 720/1392) = 0.517 → 0.5 ✓.
3. **Pattern phase pin (`PATTERN_ORIGIN_X`):** moving the anchor 1280 → 1920 shifts every tiled pattern relative to the machine (skyline sf .3 → 192 px,
   pylons sf .55 → 352 px, ground 128 px) and could re-pick the pylon a feed cable attaches to. Shift strips/ground by the C2 formula → eras I–IV backgrounds,
   ground and pylon cables are identical at the same anchor-relative camera. Dust x range anchor ± 1280 keeps density.
4. **Framing regression (computed with the real fitView, scratch script):** s0–s10 zoom + anchor-relative centre identical (pinned by a unit test).
   Zoom-0.5 stages change because the old 2560×1440 world clamp forced the centre: s11 ground screen y 560 → 480 (top 322 → 242); s13 ground 560 → 640
   (top 90 → 170, now clear of the HUD); era V full ground 661 / top 51 (= ART §5). Accepted; Art Director checks s11/s13/s15 screenshots, no compensation code.
5. **Bead depth:** the animator sets a bead's depth on a front/back change and reports it; MachineView re-sorts `parts` only on such frames (≤ 8 per orbit set).
6. **Inversion ghost angle = mirrorAngle(displayed hand-crank angle)** (= +1.75·θM when the crank offset is 0; stays mirrored after gear-up). Shown only for
   3 s after a MANUAL click (auto-clicks emit no event, D2 of WP10), alpha hard steps 1 / .66 / .33; it flies in once on purchase like any part.
7. **Sky depths:** skyViolet +1, eclipse +2, horizonGlow +3 (ART §7 had glow +2; eclipse must sit over the violet). WP12 puts skyRestored at +4.
8. **Name plate always visible** (ART §4): the only intended era I–IV pixel change (40×11 on the girder face, frame 0 `???????`). Its frame lags the HUD
   plaque by one keystone (physical relic) — accepted.
9. **Reduced-motion clamps** (ART §6) deferred to the settings WP (as WP10 D4). CR-I tint off, CR-J rejected, CR-K → WP12. Palette ramps sky/green → WP12.
10. **Roadmap effects kept exactly; only costs scaled.** inversionCrank × servo auto-clicks is the era's load-bearing jump (3 s echo × ≤ 10 auto-cranks/s),
    graviticAnchor is the old-resource (ME) sink that gates skyAperture.
11. **Perf:** RT budget (≤ 4 ms/frame machine update) re-checked by a smoke probe now (s13 vs s15); full budget pass stays WP16. Lever if over: none in WP11
    (report numbers; `MACHINE_RENDER.mode` stays 'renderTexture').
